ITP 2027

18th Conference on Interactive Theorem Proving

Summer 2027 - University of Strathclyde (Glasgow, Scotland)

Welcome!

The 18th International Conference on Interactive Theorem Proving (ITP 2027) will take place at the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow, Scotland. We have yet to finalise the dates but we expect it will be either over the last week of July or the first week of August.

ITP will be co-located with the Scottish Programming Languages and Verification summer school at the nearby Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh, Scotland.

About

ITP 2027 is part of the ITP conference series whose history goes back to 1988.

The design of the website is inspired by the Art Nouveau works of Charles Rennie Mackintosh and Margaret Macdonald. If you appreciate it, consider spending some of your time in Glasgow exploring their oeuvre.
