ITP 2027

18th Conference on Interactive Theorem Proving

University of Strathclyde (Glasgow, Scotland)

August 2nd - 7th 2027

Co-located with the SPLV summer school at Heriot-Watt University (Edinburgh, Scotland)

Call for Workshop and Tutorial Proposals

Conference website

The ITP conference organisers are soliciting proposals for affiliated workshops and tutorials. The ITP conference series is concerned with all aspects of interactive theorem proving, ranging from theoretical foundations to implementation aspects and applications in program verification, security, and the formalization of mathematics. Members of the community are invited to submit proposals on any topic related to the conference.

Workshops typically feature presentations of ongoing research that is not ready yet for formal publication, and tutorials may e.g. present extended demos of particular software.

The ITP organisers encourage a broad interpretation of the above formats and welcome proposals for any kind of event that benefits the ITP community. The workshops and tutorials will take place on the 2nd and 7th of August.

Workshop/tutorial proposals should contain the following data:

  • Name of the workshop/tutorial.
  • Brief description of the workshop/tutorial, including workshop topics.
  • Previous instances of the event (if any).
  • Contact information and affiliation of the workshop/tutorial organisers.
  • An estimate of the audience size.
  • Proposed format of the workshop/tutorial (e.g. regular talks, tool demos, poster presentations, etc.).
  • Duration of the workshop/tutorial (e.g. full-day or half-day) and the preferred day. We cannot guarantee that preferences can be followed.
  • Special technical or AV needs (if any).

All proposals should be submitted by e-mail to: CIS_itp2027@groups.strath.ac.uk.

The deadline for submitting a proposal is Thursday 14th January 2027. Earlier submissions are encouraged, and notifications of acceptance will be issued on a rolling basis.

Organizers of accepted workshops have been invited by the managing editor of the Electronic Notes in Theoretical and Applied Informatics and Computer Science (ENTICS) to consider publishing their Proceedings, if any, in the series (which is fully open access, without publishing charges, and indexed by DBLP).
