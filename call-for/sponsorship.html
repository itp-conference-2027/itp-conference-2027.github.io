ITP 2027

18th Conference on Interactive Theorem Proving

University of Strathclyde (Glasgow, Scotland)

August 2nd - 7th 2027

Co-located with the SPLV summer school at Heriot-Watt University (Edinburgh, Scotland)

Why ITP 2027

The Interactive Theorem Proving conference series brings together researchers interested in all aspects of interactive theorem proving. It covers topics ranging from theoretical foundations to implementation aspects; applications to program verification, security; and the formalisation of mathematics, and dates back as far as 1988.

For more information about ITP, please visit: https://itp-conference.github.io/

Scotland is internationally renowned for its leading expertise in programming language design, implementation, and formal program verification. Within Scotland, the Scottish Programming Languages Institute (SPLI) unites researchers in programming languages research from the Universities of Edinburgh, Glasgow, Heriot-Watt, St Andrews, Stirling, Strathclyde, and West of Scotland. Together, the SPLI communities co-ordinates events across Scotland which enhance programming languages research and knowledge exchange.

By sponsoring ITP 2027, your organisation will gain access not only to the ITP communities, but also to the SPLI communities that contain experts at the cutting edge of programming languages and verification research. Your organisation will have the chance to collaborate and engage with these communities, as well as access to the communities’ postgraduate students.

Through sponsoring ITP 2027 your company’s funds will help to lower participation costs, and support the running of the conference.
